Quick Pro Tips

Battle royale

Fight smart, survive longer.

Winning isn't only about eliminations. Good positioning and teamwork can turn a tough match around.

  • 01 Take cover before healing.
  • 02 Share enemy locations with your squad.
  • 03 Rotate before the zone gets tight.
  • 04 Don't chase every fight.
Aim routine

Warm up before ranked.

Keep practice short, repeatable and comfortable for your controls.

  • 01 Start with a few minutes of target tracking.
  • 02 Practise controlled flicks, not random swipes.
  • 03 Change one sensitivity setting at a time.
  • 04 Review what worked after each match.
